Continuity is a CONSTRUCTION guarantee (Prop 1: time-independent sum weights) -- every PC variant is a valid continuous-time flow, ~1e-7, trained or not. What velocity matching buys is TEACHER-PATH FIDELITY: the path-trained variants (marginal_path_vel, pair_path) match the teacher's velocity at tau<1; the endpoint-only variants do not. This is velocity's real role -- separate from UQ (density) and from continuity (structural). No contradiction with the doc.
